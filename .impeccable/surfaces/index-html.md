# Hello Duang home and Word Bank

## Scope

Homepage `index.html` (persuade) and English Word Bank `eng/wordbank_az.html` (operate). One visual system. Other lesson pages are out of scope except their links.

## Audience and job

Someone in Duang’s house opens the site on a phone or laptop, picks a lesson, and listens. The Word Bank is the review course before a spelling check.

## Constraints

Static files only. Keep lesson hrefs, titles, dates, previews, the Tone Perfect credit, audio paths, and `#LA`–`#LZ`. Pixel art only from the family set. Public name Duang / 端端 only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A listening library that opens like a course home and plays like a lesson, on frosted glass over morning window light. It refuses the game-card catalog, the rainbow letter bar, the mascot, and a marketing split of headline beside a fake device.

OWN-WORLD: Cool mist ground with one warm sun pool and one cool sky pool. Panels are translucent white, blur plus saturate, a dark hairline, a soft offset shadow, and a large radius; they turn solid when blur is missing. One sapphire accent. System stack only. Family pixel art sits in a white tile; a missing picture is a light-weight letter on glass, same frame.

STORY: The first screen says the pages are here to be heard, offers 开始学习, and shows a continue instrument with real progress from this device. Subjects lead into every existing lesson. The Word Bank is a player: letter units, a word, its sound.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width headline 点开就能听。 with 听 in the accent, one supporting sentence, and the solid 开始学习 control on that same band. Under it, a full-width glass continue instrument: progress text, a meter, a link back to the last letter, and one real seal card that plays. Not a two-column mockup. Signature interaction: the playing card draws an accent ring, and the active control shows three bars; the picture and 正常 both start normal speed.

FORM: Owner-pinned. Apple frosted glass, system type, one accent, the stated information architecture. Concept-seed was not rolled because the brief pinned the direction. Seed key: brief-pinned.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
